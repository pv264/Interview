I remember it with four simple questions. SLI tells me how the service is actually performing. SLO tells me what reliability target we've set internally. SLA is the commitment we've made to customers and may include penalties if it's not met. The Error Budget is the amount of failure we're allowed while still meeting our SLO. We use the Error Budget to decide whether to continue releasing new features or temporarily focus on improving system reliability. If plenty of budget remains, we continue deployments. If the budget is almost exhausted, we slow or pause releases until the service becomes stable again."
